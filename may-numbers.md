## What the data actually says

**1. The Top Joke Series is not the strongest format.** May topped out at 1,200
views. Every post above 1,200 this year came from the daily series, and the best
did 2.9K. Thirty more Top Joke Series videos is doubling down on the weaker of
the two things this account does.

**2. Views are the wrong metric here.** The May post with the fewest reactions
(12) and nearly the fewest views (635) got **14 comments** — three to seven times
any other post that month. That was the cross-show Comedy WAR leaderboard,
George Costanza at #1 across 59,000 jokes and six sitcoms. People did not
watch it, they argued with it.

The next two by engagement rate say the same thing: "Community's last truly
great episode is in Season 5" at 11.78% and "The Iannucci truthers are not going
to like this one" at 9.74%. Both are contestable claims. Neither is a clip with
a score on it.

**3. The "Fight me." card is the right instinct attached to the wrong content.**
A single joke with a 9.57 on it is not arguable — nobody has a prior about
whether "He's gonna Jack!" is a 9.57. A ranking is arguable, because everybody
already has an opinion about who is funnier.

## Recommendation

Let the 23 scheduled videos run. They are built, loaded and cost nothing more,
and 23 consecutive days of one format at one time is the clean experiment this
account has never actually run. Read it on Oct 1, on comments per post, not views.

**Do not pull the six remaining clips.** That is the most expensive work left
(sourcing, timestamping, rendering, roughly an hour each) on the format the data
already ranks second.

**Spend that time on cross-show leaderboard posts instead.** They need no clip
sourcing at all, they are what produced the highest comment count of the year,
and they are the thing only this dataset can make. Nobody else can post "we
scored 173,000 jokes across 21 shows and here is who actually wins."

**And fix the WAR floor before doing that.** Leaderboard posts drive traffic to
the character rankings, which is the exact page showing Rachel Green at 0.0,
ranked below three characters with five jokes each. The cosmetic bug is sitting
on the landing page for the content that works.
